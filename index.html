Label scan

Read any ingredients panel in one photo.

Optical character recognition pulls the text out of your photo, then repairs the mistakes small print reliably causes — so every way a label can name the same additive ends up as one finding.

  • ✓ It reads the photo on your device Tesseract.js does the recognition inside this browser, so nothing is uploaded and a weak supermarket connection does not matter.
  • ✓ A claim is not an ingredient “No added sugar” and “unsweetened” are read as negated phrases, so they never get counted as sugar on the label.
  • ✓ Print too small to photograph Type or paste the ingredients text from the pack instead and the report comes out the same.

How it works

Three steps, about twenty seconds.

Everything runs locally, which is also why it works on a shaky connection in the middle of a supermarket aisle.

  1. Set your conditions

    Tick what applies to you beside the scanner. Create a profile to have it remembered across scans, or stay a guest.

  2. Photograph the label

    Point at the ingredients panel and take the shot, or drop in an image you already have. Good light and a flat surface do most of the work.

  3. Read the report

    Personal warnings first, then the kids rating, then every flagged additive with its risks. Each scan is saved to your history.

Don't just read the label

Ingredient_Check in many more ways

Grades the risk, with reasons

Each finding carries a severity, what the additive actually does in food, and what regular consumption is associated with — an explanation, not a scary word.

Matches your own conditions

Tick diabetes, blood pressure, heart, kidney, gut, asthma or PCOS and the report leads with the ingredients you were specifically told to avoid, and why.

Suggests a simpler swap

When a product looks heavily processed, name it and get alternatives that satisfy the same craving with a shorter ingredients list.

Conditions

Eight avoid lists, drawn from what people are actually told.

Each condition has its own list of the ingredients, preservatives and colours that turn up most often in packaged groceries — with a reason attached to every group.

Kids safety

Every product gets an age, not a sticker.

Kids safety runs on every single scan, whether or not the pack is aimed at children. The additives found on the label set a minimum age, and each one explains itself — so you can decide, rather than take the app's word for it.

    Your scans do not leave this browser

    Photos are processed on your device and thrown away. History, conditions and any profile you create are stored in this browser's local storage — which also means clearing site data wipes them, and they will not follow you to another computer.
